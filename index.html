📖 Singer's Guide & 22-Shruti Science

🎯 The Analog Arc Dial

The curved needle swings along a 100° arc from -50¢ (flat) to +50¢ (sharp). The center 0° tick is pure integer acoustic harmony. When your voice enters the glowing emerald lock zone, you achieve true resonance with the tanpura.

🌿 Why Shrutis Differ From Western Tuners

Western piano and guitar tuners enforce 12-Tone Equal Temperament (12-TET). Indian Classical Music is rooted in 5-limit Just Intonation. For example, Shuddha Gandhar in Bhoopali (5/4) is 13.7¢ flatter than a keyboard tuner, producing a sweet, beatless harmonic lock.
